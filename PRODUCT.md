# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Literary editors, workshop directors, fellowship committees, and fellow writers
visiting the personal site of **Francesca Olarte**, an incoming fourth-year
Creative Writing student. They arrive to read her work, gauge her voice, and
decide whether to publish, fund, or collaborate. Context: a quiet, attentive
read — often on a laptop, sometimes a phone — closer to opening a journal than
scanning a résumé.

## Product Purpose

A personal literary site that presents Francesca's writing (short stories,
poetry, essays, screenplays) alongside publications, awards, a journal/blog, and
a résumé. Success is a visitor who finishes the scroll feeling they have *read
something*, not browsed a template — and who remembers the site afterward.

## Brand Personality

Literary, dreamy, composed. Voice: intimate and precise, never precious. The
site should read like a well-set literary magazine — confident negative space,
considered pacing, a single sustained mood (soft floral dusk) carried through a
cinematic scroll rather than a stack of boxed sections.

## Anti-references

- Generic developer/agency portfolio templates (card grid of "projects").
- SaaS landing tropes: hero-metric blocks, identical icon-heading-text cards,
  tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows above every section.
- Stacked, visually-identical sections with hard seams between them.
- Glassmorphism-everywhere. Glass is used only where a surface must float over
  imagery.

## Design Principles

1. **The page is one story, not seven sections.** Transitions matter as much as
   the sections; the seams should dissolve.
2. **Show the writing, don't describe it.** Real excerpts and typographic care
   over labels and chrome.
3. **One sustained atmosphere.** A floral-dusk mood that evolves with scroll
   instead of being confined to the hero.
4. **Editorial restraint with signature moments.** Mostly calm and readable, with
   2–3 deliberate cinematic peaks that earn memory.
5. **Asymmetry over centering.** Magazine composition, not centered stacks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast. All scroll-driven motion,
smooth-scroll, and parallax must fully degrade under
`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` to instant, legible, static layout. Keyboard
and screen-reader order follow the visual narrative; decorative petals and
folios are `aria-hidden`.
